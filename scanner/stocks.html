A screen built from SEC filings, not advice and not a recommendation to buy or sell anything. It can only measure what is in the numbers. Filings contain errors, and a high score says nothing about the future.
Eight checks drawn from what Berkshire Hathaway's shareholder letters keep returning to: steady earnings, high returns on capital with little debt, profit that turns into cash for owners, and kept profits that earn their keep. "Price / value" compares the market price with a conservative estimate (owner earnings growing at half their past rate, discounted at 10%). Under 1.0 means the price is below that estimate.
Click a column heading to sort. Leave a box empty to ignore it. A company with no market value (share count could not be trusted) only shows under "Any size".
